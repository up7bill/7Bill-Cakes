Behind 7Bill Cakes

Meet The Bakersman.

7Bill Cakes is a custom cake business specializing in cakes, cupcakes, and other cake-based desserts. I focus on creating visually appealing, high-quality desserts for birthdays, graduations, holidays, celebrations, and everything in between even “just because.”

My passion for baking comes from family and the memories I made baking with my grandma Joan. What started as a personal hobby eventually grew into 7Bill Cakes through word of mouth, repeat customers, and a growing demand for custom creations. Today, I bring together my love for baking, creativity, cake artistry, and digital design to make each order feel unique.

As 7Bill Cakes continues to grow, my goal is to build a recognizable custom cake brand known for creative designs, memorable customer experiences, and strong visual presentation while never losing the love & family-centered foundation that inspired me to start baking in the first place.
